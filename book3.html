1984

Author: George Orwell

Published: June 8, 1949

Genre: Dystopian Science fiction

Description: Among the seminal texts of the 20th century, Nineteen Eighty-Four is a rare work that grows more haunting as its futuristic purgatory becomes more real. Published in 1949, the book offers political satirist George Orwell's nightmarish vision of a totalitarian, bureaucratic world.
